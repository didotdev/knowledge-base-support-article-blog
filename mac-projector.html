How to Connect a Mac to a Projector

November 7, 2026 5 min read By DevBase Support

Connect your Mac to a projector using an HDMI cable or wireless screen mirroring. Then choose whether to duplicate your Mac screen or use the projector as a second display.

1. Connect with a cable

  1. Turn on the projector and select its HDMI input.
  2. Connect an HDMI cable between the projector and your Mac. If your Mac does not have an HDMI port, use a compatible USB-C or Thunderbolt-to-HDMI adapter.
  3. Open the Apple menu, choose System Settings, then select Displays. Your Mac should detect the projector automatically.

If your projector uses VGA or another input, choose an adapter that supports both your Mac's port and the projector's connection. Check the adapter's specifications for supported resolution.

2. Choose mirroring or an extended desktop

In System Settings > Displays, select the projector and choose the display arrangement you want. Mirroring shows the same image on both screens; an extended desktop gives you extra workspace. When using an extended desktop, arrange the display icons to match the physical positions of your screens.

Available controls vary by macOS version. If you see a Use as menu, use it to select mirroring or an extended display.

3. Connect wirelessly

If the projector or a connected Apple TV supports AirPlay, connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network. Open Control Center from the menu bar, select Screen Mirroring, and choose the projector or Apple TV. Follow any on-screen prompt to enter a passcode.

4. Set the sound output if needed

If you want audio to come from the projector or its speakers, open System Settings > Sound > Output and select the projector or connected audio device, if it appears. Some projectors do not support audio over their video connection, so you may need to keep using your Mac's speakers or connect separate speakers.

Troubleshooting

  • No picture: Confirm the projector's selected input matches the port you connected, then unplug and reconnect the cable or adapter.
  • Projector not listed: Open Displays settings again. For AirPlay, check that both devices support it and are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Blurry or cropped image: In Displays settings, try a resolution supported by the projector and check its aspect-ratio or scaling options.
  • No sound: Choose an available output in Sound settings and verify that the projector's volume is turned up.
Before your presentation: Connect the Mac, select the correct projector input, and test the picture and sound before you begin.
