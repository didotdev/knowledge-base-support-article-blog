Webhook delivery failures: a playbook for recovery

April 26, 2026 7 min read By Integrations Team

A step-by-step guide to diagnosing retries, dead letters, and downstream delivery drift.

Webhook failures are rarely a single bug. They are usually a chain of delivery attempts, timeout policies, retry windows, and contract mismatches.

Start by classifying the failure: is it a receiver rejection, a timeout, or a dead-letter event after repeated retries?

Retries can create duplicate events if downstream systems are not idempotent. Add an event ID, replay window, and delivery state to make deduplication reliable.

Tracking retry backoff and logging attempt count makes the difference between a temporary incident and a long-running customer problem.

The best recovery strategies reduce noise, preserve idempotency, and improve the feedback loop between platform and client integrations.
